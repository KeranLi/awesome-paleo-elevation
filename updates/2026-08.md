# August 2026 Update

## Scope of the seed list

The initial collection contains papers published from 2022 to 2026, plus selected foundational reviews. Discovery terms included paleo-elevation, paleoaltimetry, paleotopography, Tibetan Plateau, Himalaya, stable isotopes, clumped isotopes, volcanic glass, leaf wax, pollen, thermochronology, and uplift history.

This is a curated seed list, not a systematic review. Chinese databases and records not indexed by CrossRef require separate manual review.

## Themes to watch

### Archive screening before elevation inference

Recent carbonate studies increasingly combine clumped-isotope temperatures, U-Pb dating, petrography, and stable isotopes. Their shared message is that archive preservation must be established before an elevation estimate can be trusted.

### Moisture pathways as an explicit model variable

Modern river-water, leaf-wax, and precipitation studies show that sub-cloud evaporation, recycling, snowmelt, and transport pathways can alter isotope-elevation relationships. A single universal isotope lapse rate should not be applied without a regional moisture-source assessment.

### Integrated regional reconstructions

Recent Tibetan studies combine geochronology, detrital provenance, stable and clumped isotopes, geomorphology, and palaeodrainage. This produces more useful regional constraints but also makes age alignment and proxy representativeness central review questions.

### Biological constraints

Plant communities, pollen, and fossil assemblages provide independent constraints on paleo-elevation and topography. They should be assessed alongside their ecological assumptions and preservation bias rather than treated as simple substitutes for isotope proxies.

## Review checklist

For each paper, record:

- what geographic and topographic quantity is reconstructed;
- whether the proxy represents local precipitation, basin floor, catchment mean, or adjacent ranges;
- chronology and temporal resolution;
- calibration assumptions and quantitative uncertainty;
- moisture-pathway, diagenetic, source, and spatial-representativeness caveats;
- whether the stated finding is an observation, a model inference, or a synthesis.
