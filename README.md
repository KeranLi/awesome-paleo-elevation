# Awesome Paleo-Elevation [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of papers and resources on paleo-elevation, paleoaltimetry, and paleotopography.

This repository is an English-language, community-oriented reading list for reconstructing past elevations and topography. It covers proxies, calibration studies, uncertainty analysis, and regional reconstructions, with an emphasis on the Tibetan Plateau and the Himalaya.

The public repository contains bibliographic records and concise, source-aware reading summaries. Copyrighted PDFs and working notes remain local and are excluded from Git.

## Scope

- Paleo-elevation, paleoaltimetry, and paleotopography reconstructions.
- Proxy calibration, preservation screening, and uncertainty assessment.
- Surface uplift, exhumation, and paleo-drainage studies where they constrain elevation history.
- Links between topographic evolution and climate, ecosystems, or mineral preservation.

## Inclusion Criteria

Each entry must have a stable bibliographic source, preferably a DOI, and a clear connection to paleo-elevation or paleotopography. The list distinguishes records awaiting review from papers that have been read and summarized by a maintainer.

## Local Reading Workspace

PDFs belong in `_downloads/` and personal reading notes belong in `_private_notes/`. Both directories are ignored by Git. After reading a paper, add a concise, evidence-based public entry to [papers.md](papers.md) and optionally a detailed English summary under [notes/](notes/).

## Contributing

Please read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Do not submit copyrighted PDFs. Keep claims attributable to the cited paper and identify important limitations or unresolved uncertainty.
