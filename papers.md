## Foundations and reviews

- [Stable isotope paleoaltimetry: A critical review](https://doi.org/10.2475/ajs.305.10.1033) - Blisniuk (2005). *American Journal of Science*. Global; stable-isotope paleoaltimetry; critical review. **to-read**
- [Reconstructing paleoelevation in eroded orogens](https://doi.org/10.1130/g20394.1) - Mulch et al. (2004). *Geology*. Global orogens; isotopes, thermochronology, and exhumation; reconstruction framework. **to-read**
- [Stable Isotope-Based Paleoaltimetry: Theory and Validation](https://doi.org/10.2138/rmg.2007.66.2) - Rowley (2007). *Reviews in Mineralogy and Geochemistry*. Global; stable-isotope theory and validation. **to-read**
- [Paleoelevation Reconstruction using Pedogenic Carbonates](https://doi.org/10.2138/rmg.2007.66.3) - Quade, Garzione, and Eiler (2007). *Reviews in Mineralogy and Geochemistry*. Global; pedogenic carbonates and stable isotopes. **to-read**
- [Paleoaltimetry reconstructions of the Tibetan Plateau: progress and contradictions](https://doi.org/10.1093/nsr/nwv062) - Deng and Ding (2015). *National Science Review*. Tibetan Plateau; multi-proxy review of Cenozoic uplift. **to-read**

- [Reliability of micritic carbonates in recording well-preserved isotopic composition](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gca.2024.04.009) - Li et al. (2024). *Geochimica et Cosmochimica Acta*. Central Tibet; micritic-carbonate preservation and isotope reliability. **to-read**
- [Quantifying Elevation Effects on d13C, d18O, and Clumped Isotope (Delta47)](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025jf008315) - Kong et al. (2025). *Journal of Geophysical Research: Earth Surface*. Southeastern Tibetan Plateau; modern carbonate transect from 0 to 3,500 m. **to-read**
- [Moisture Recycling Modulates Stable Isotopes of River Water](https://doi.org/10.1175/jhm-d-24-0155.1) - Lu et al. (2025). *Journal of Hydrometeorology*. Bailong River, northeastern Tibetan Plateau; river-water isotopes and moisture recycling. **to-read**
- [Validating the potential application of d2Hwax and soil brGDGTs](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.quascirev.2023.108306) - Li et al. (2023). *Quaternary Science Reviews*. Southern Himalaya; leaf-wax d2H and soil brGDGT calibration. **to-read**

## Tibetan Plateau and Himalaya

- [Orographic evolution of northern Tibet shaped vegetation and plant diversity in eastern Asia](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abc7741) - Li et al. (2021). *Science Advances*. Northern Tibet and eastern Asia; topographic and climate modelling linked to vegetation and plant diversity. **to-read**
- [Palaeo-altimetry of the late Eocene to Miocene Lunpola basin, central Tibet](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature04506) - Rowley and Currie (2006). *Nature*. Lunpola Basin, central Tibet; carbonate oxygen isotopes and hypsometric paleoaltimetry. Central Tibet exceeded 4 km by the late Eocene to early Oligocene (approximately 35 ± 5 Ma); the estimate may be a lower bound and can represent a precipitation-weighted catchment. **read** ([note](notes/rowley_currie2006_lunpola_paleoaltimetry.md))
- [Cenozoic paleoelevation history of the Lunpola Basin in Central Tibet](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.earscirev.2022.104068) - Li et al. (2022). *Earth-Science Reviews*. Lunpola Basin, central Tibet; volcanic-glass dD and critical proxy review. Early Miocene precipitation-weighted catchment elevation estimated at 4.2-4.9 km; proxy interpretation depends strongly on moisture pathways, rain-shadow effects, and the water source recorded. **read** ([note](notes/li2022_lunpola_review.md))
- [Eocene to Miocene paleoclimate reconstruction of the northern Tibetan Plateau](https://doi.org/10.3389/feart.2023.1217304) - Li et al. (2023). *Frontiers in Earth Science*. Western Qaidam Basin; evaporite mineralogy and lacustrine-carbonate carbon and oxygen isotopes. Aridification is linked to Paratethys retreat, regional uplift and denudation, and later global cooling; the study provides climate context rather than a single elevation estimate. **read** ([note](notes/li2023_qaidam_paleoclimate.md))
- [Middle Miocene paleoenvironmental change and paleoelevation of the Lunpola Basin](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gloplacha.2022.104009) - Sun et al. (2023). *Global and Planetary Change*. Lunpola Basin; middle Miocene paleoenvironment and paleo-elevation. **to-read**
- [Inversion of Late Miocene uplift history from the transient Daxia River landscape](https://doi.org/10.1144/jgs2023-030) - Zhang et al. (2023). *Journal of the Geological Society*. Northeastern Tibetan Plateau; late Miocene channel-profile and landscape inversion. **to-read**
- [Revisiting the mechanisms of mid-Tertiary uplift of the NE Tibetan Plateau](https://doi.org/10.1093/nsr/nwad008) - Wei et al. (2023). *National Science Review*. West Qinling, Hoh Xil, and Qaidam; stratigraphy, sedimentology, and volcanism. **to-read**
- [Research progress on the age, paleoenvironment, and paleo-elevation of Cenozoic strata in the Lunpola Basin](https://doi.org/10.1360/tb-2023-0653) - Sun et al. (2023). *Chinese Science Bulletin*. Lunpola Basin; Cenozoic stratigraphy, paleoenvironment, and paleo-elevation review. **to-read**
- [Insights into paleoelevation and paleo-drainage change in northern Tibet](https://doi.org/10.1130/b38115.1) - Zeng et al. (2025). *Geological Society of America Bulletin*. Fenghuoshan Basin; Late Cretaceous to late Oligocene geochronology and isotopes. **to-read**
- [Integrating fossil and extant plant communities to calibrate paleoelevation](https://doi.org/10.1111/jse.13172) - Lai et al. (2025). *Journal of Systematics and Evolution*. Qinghai-Tibet Plateau; fossil and extant plant-community calibration. **to-read**
- [Stable isotopes of modern water and biotic fossils across central Himalaya](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.palaeo.2025.113256) - Xu et al. (2025). *Palaeogeography, Palaeoclimatology, Palaeoecology*. Central Himalaya; modern-water and biotic-fossil stable isotopes. **to-read**
- [Late Cretaceous-Paleogene Exhumation History and Evolution of Paleotopography in the Gaize Basin](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsomega.4c10175) - Gao et al. (2025). *ACS Omega*. Gaize Basin, central Tibet; exhumation history and paleotopography. **to-read**
- [Paleoelevation variations and exhumation history of the Tibetan Plateau](https://doi.org/10.1130/b38560.1) - Zhang et al. (2026). *Geological Society of America Bulletin*. Tibetan Plateau; tectonic evolution, paleoelevation, exhumation, and porphyry deposits. **to-read**
- [Late Miocene pollen record and quantitative paleoclimate and paleoelevation reconstructions](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.palaeo.2026.114135) - Zhang et al. (2026). *Palaeogeography, Palaeoclimatology, Palaeoecology*. Jianzha Basin, northeastern Tibetan Plateau; pollen-based reconstruction. **to-read**

## Other regions and transferable cases

- [Tectonic extension and paleoelevation influence mammalian diversity dynamics](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adn6842) - Smiley et al. (2024). *Science Advances*. Basin and Range, western North America; fossil mammals and geophysical topographic-evolution models. **to-read**
- [Paleoaltimetry of intracontinental mountains: The uplift history of the Wallowa Mountains](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chemgeo.2025.123087) - Vialichka and Bindeman (2025). *Chemical Geology*. Wallowa Mountains, Oregon; triple oxygen and hydrogen isotopes in meteoric hydrothermal systems. **to-read**
- [Orographic precipitation, fossil leaf wax d2H, and the paleotopography of the Eocene Sierra Nevada](https://doi.org/10.1130/ges02851.1) - Hren and Brandon (2026). *Geosphere*. Sierra Nevada, California; fossil leaf-wax d2H and orographic precipitation modelling. **to-read**
